NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the two categories?

Conference registration is ₹5,000 per participant for attending sessions. Hackathon registration is ₹7,500 per team for team participation. Both amounts are proposed and must be confirmed.

Is ₹7,500 charged per team member?

No. The current concept lists ₹7,500 as the fee for one team entry. The proposed team size is 2–4 members. Final rules must be published by the organizers.

Are the prizes guaranteed?

No. The prize amounts shown are illustrative proposal values and should not be treated as confirmed until the organizers publish official terms.

Does submitting this form register me?

No. This is a static demo. It does not save or transmit information. A real registration platform, privacy notice and payment provider must be connected before launch.

Is this an official IIIT Hyderabad website?

No. This is a design prototype for a proposed event. Institutional branding, venue, event details, dates and any official affiliation must be authorized before publication.
